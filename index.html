Mile 1

About

Hi, I'm Aubrey — a high school student in Surrey, BC with a growing obsession with computers, tinkering, and figuring out how things work underneath. I'm building toward studying computer science at the Paul G. Allen School at the University of Washington, and along the way I'm laying down a real foundation in algorithms, software engineering, and applied machine learning. Outside of code, I'm writing a novella set during the First World War, about the brutal realities faced by the people who lived through it.

Why CS, why now

I've spent the last few years experimenting with computers, small projects, and whatever ideas I could get my hands on — and somewhere along the way I realized this is the work that actually makes me feel engaged. I'm not choosing computer science because it's easy; it won't be. It's the one path where I can see myself growing, learning, and building something meaningful. I'm still early and still figuring things out, but I'm committed to the foundation: algorithms, programming, problem-solving, and the discipline it takes to turn curiosity into skill.

Why UW, why Seattle

The University of Washington feels like the right place for me — not only because of the Paul G. Allen School and the opportunities it offers, but because I want to build a life in the Pacific Northwest. The mountains, the evergreen landscape, the atmosphere around Seattle all feel like where I want to grow into adulthood. UW is the stepping stone: a place to challenge myself academically, go deeper into computer science, and eventually settle into a stable, meaningful career built around work I actually care about.
